FRAME

Apps become optional

FRAME turns your computer from a collection of apps into a collection of things.

Today, computers work like this

Open Photoshop

↓

Edit image

↓

Save file

↓

Upload to Google Drive

↓

Share in Discord

↓

Message someone

Everything is trapped inside separate apps.

FRAME works differently

Instead of opening an app… you interact directly with the thing itself.

For example, imagine you have a project.

Instead of

  • Slack
  • GitHub
  • Google Drive
  • Notion
  • ChatGPT
  • Dropbox

You simply have

Project Mercury

Click it.

Inside it are:

Project Mercury

  • Files
  • AI
  • Notes
  • Team
  • Permissions
  • History
  • Tasks
  • Storage
  • Wallet

Everything related to that project. They’re all part of one object.

Think of LEGO

Every piece in FRAME is a building block.

  • A file is a block.
  • A folder is a block.
  • A person is a block.
  • An AI is a block.
  • A website is a block.
  • A company is a block.
  • A permission is a block.

You snap them together however you want.

The biggest difference

Today your computer asks

Which app do you want to use?

FRAME asks

What are you trying to do?

Instead of

“Open Photoshop.”

You say

“Edit this image.”

Instead of

“Open Google Drive.”

You say

“Share this with Sarah.”

Instead of

“Open Settings.”

You say

“Let my AI access the camera.”

You work with your goals—not with applications.

AI becomes part of the computer

Today AI is another website or another app.

In FRAME, AI is just another helper that lives with your data.

Imagine opening a project and seeing:

Project Mercury

  • Files
  • Website
  • Notes

↓

AI

That AI already knows everything about that project because it lives there.

You don’t have to upload files or explain context every time.

Nothing is hidden

Every object can explain itself.

Imagine clicking your Wi-Fi connection. Instead of seeing confusing settings, it says:

Connected because:

  • Password is correct.
  • Signal is strong.
  • Router is available.

Or if something doesn’t work:

Camera unavailable because:

  • Another app is using it.

The computer explains why, not just what.

Everything has history

Imagine accidentally deleting something.

Instead of panicking, you simply move backward through its history.

You can see:

  • what changed
  • when it changed
  • why it changed
  • who changed it

Almost like Google Docs history—but for everything on your computer.

Everything is connected

Instead of searching through folders, you can ask:

“Show me everything related to my website.”

FRAME instantly shows:

  • the website
  • the domain
  • the SSL certificate
  • the AI that built it
  • the server
  • the storage
  • the people working on it
  • the deployment history

Because it understands relationships, not just filenames.

One sentence

If I had to explain FRAME to someone in a coffee shop, I’d say:

FRAME is a computer where everything is an intelligent object that knows what it is, how it relates to everything else, why it’s in its current state, and can work together with every other object—without being trapped inside separate apps.

That’s really the core idea. Apps become optional. The focus shifts from launching software to interacting directly with the things you care about.
